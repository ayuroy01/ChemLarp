Molecule

Type a SMILES string. OmniChem checks it, writes its canonical form and works out formula, mass and stereochemistry.

3D model

Builds 3D coordinates with all hydrogens (distance geometry), relaxes them with the UFF force field and checks that the stereochemistry matches. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Build a structure to see it here.

Reaction

Write a reaction with SMILES and coefficients separated by spaces. OmniChem checks that every element and charge balances and gives ΔH°, ΔS°, ΔG° and K.

Equilibrium

Lists the gas-phase species that may form, one per line as SMILES moles. OmniChem minimises the Gibbs energy at the given temperature and pressure.

Kinetics

Runs a batch reactor with a reaction mechanism. Use the built-in hydrogen–oxygen example or load your own (.ocm, CHEMKIN chem.inp or Cantera YAML).

Phases

Vapour–liquid equilibrium from a cubic equation of state. List the mixture, one per line as SMILES mole_fraction, and OmniChem tests whether it splits into liquid and vapour and finds both compositions.

Vapour pressure of a pure compound

Built-in critical constants cover about 30 common compounds (light hydrocarbons, aromatics, alcohols, water, CO₂, N₂, O₂, H₂…). Peng–Robinson is typically within 1–2% for non-polar compounds; for water and alcohols expect a few percent.
